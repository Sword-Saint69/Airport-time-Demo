## Option 2 — a little more story

> There's a video floating around of an airport departure board showing the local time of every city in the world — so you never miss your flight.
>
> That's [HASSCO© (@itshassco)](https://x.com/itshassco/status/2106761271282737292)'s idea, and it's a good one.
>
> So I built it. ✈️
>
> ⚡️ An LED departure board where the clock characters physically split-flap over when the minute changes
> 🗺️ A dot-matrix world map that shows where it's night right now
> 🌍 Live times for 227 cities, a 12/24-hour toggle, and a board you can reorder
> ⌨️ Keyboard-first — `?` shows every shortcut
> 🎨 Five palettes, an installable PWA, and preferences that survive a reload
>
> Built with Next.js 16, React 19 and Tailwind v4. Open source:
>
> github.com/Sword-Saint69/Airport-time-Demo
>
> Would love to hear what else you'd put on the board. 🛫
